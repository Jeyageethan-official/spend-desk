Privacy Policy for SpendDesk

Last updated: September 27, 2026

At SpendDesk ("we", "our", or "us"), available at https://jeyageethan-official.github.io/spend-desk/, user privacy is paramount. This Privacy Policy details how we handle user data and protect your financial tracking information.

1. Data Collection and Usage

SpendDesk is a privacy-first spending tracker. By default, all financial transaction data, category definitions, and budgets are saved locally within your browser's Local Storage.

2. Google Account Data & OAuth Scopes

If you choose to sign in with Google to enable Google Sheets sync, SpendDesk requests access to the following Google OAuth scopes:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/spreadsheets - To create and update your connected SpendDesk Google Spreadsheet.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file - To view and manage Google Spreadsheets created or selected by this app.

How we use Google Data: Google tokens and Spreadsheet IDs are used strictly to synchronize your personal transactions and dashboard stats with your own Google Sheet. We do not store your financial data on external servers, nor do we access any unrelated files in your Google Drive.

3. Third-Party Sharing & Data Selling

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal or financial data with any third parties or advertisers.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

You can delete your local data at any time via App Settings ➔ Reset All Data. Signing out clears your stored access tokens and profile information immediately.
